    Questions people ask

    Everything about how this tracker works, in plain words.

    Is this the official Factorial Academy website?
    This is a free study tracker built around the Factorial Academy YouTube channel. Every lesson opens on YouTube. For the academy's own site, use the Website link in the Connect section above.
    Do I need an account, and does it cost anything?
    No account, no sign-up, no payment. Open the page and start ticking off videos. Everything here is free, and so are the lessons on YouTube.
    Where is my progress stored?
    In this browser only, on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. That means clearing your browser data will clear it too, so export a backup from Settings now and then, and import it on your other device.
    How does the revision schedule work?
    Star a video and it enters spaced repetition. By default it comes back after 1, 3, 7, 15 and 30 days, which is enough spacing for formulas and methods to stick. Change the gaps in Settings if you prefer a tighter or looser cycle.
    How do new uploads reach the tracker?
    Add a free YouTube Data API key in Sync and the tracker can check the channel for new videos, sort them into the right chapter and load video lengths. Without a key you can still paste links manually with Add videos.
    Can I use it offline or install it like an app?
    Yes. It ships as a Progressive Web App, so the page itself works offline once loaded. Use Install as an app in the footer, or on iPhone tap Share and then Add to Home Screen. Watching videos still needs a connection.
    How should I actually study with it?
    Pick one chapter, use Continue to keep the order, and set a small daily goal you can hit on a bad day. Star anything you had to think twice about, then clear the revision queue before starting anything new.
    Something is wrong or missing. How do I report it?
    Use the contact form above, email directly, or message on Telegram. Chapter mix-ups are the easiest thing to fix, so do send them.

      Privacy

      Short, because there is very little to say.

      What is collected

      Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no tracking pixel. No server belonging to this site ever sees you, because this site has no server. It is a single page of HTML.

      Where your progress lives

      Ticks, stars, notes, goals, theme and revision dates are saved by your browser, on the device you are using, under one key in local storage. They are never uploaded. Clearing your browser data deletes them, which is why Settings offers an export.

      What your browser talks to

      • Fonts — the typeface (Inter) is served from this site itself, so no font request ever goes to a third party.
      • YouTube — thumbnails load from i.ytimg.com and lessons stream from YouTube’s own player, embedded on this page. Google’s privacy policy applies to that playback, and you can switch to youtube-nocookie.com, or back to opening lessons on YouTube itself, in Settings → Player.
      • YouTube Data API — only if you choose to add your own API key for sync. The key is stored in your browser and sent only to Google.

      Those are ordinary requests to Google-run services and are governed by Google’s privacy policy, not this one.

      Children

      The audience here is exam candidates, many of whom are under 18. Since nothing is collected, nothing about a minor is collected either. No message can be sent from this page without your email app opening first, so you always see what is being sent.

      Changes

      If this ever stops being true, the change will appear under What’s new before it takes effect.

      Questions? Use the contact form, or write to the email in the Connect section.
