## Video hosting and data

**YouTube**, a service of **Google LLC**. Lessons stream from YouTube's
own player, embedded in the page through the **YouTube IFrame Player
API**, under Google's terms and privacy policy. Visitors can switch the
embed to youtube-nocookie.com, or go back to opening lessons on
youtube.com itself, in Settings. Thumbnails load from i.ytimg.com.

Titles, upload dates and durations are fetched from the **YouTube Data
API v3**, and only when the visitor supplies their own API key. The key
is stored in that visitor's browser and sent only to Google.

YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.

## Built with

Plain HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript. Zero dependencies, zero trackers,
zero cookies, zero third-party scripts beyond the two font files.

Standards used: Progressive Web App and service worker, Web Share API,

FAQPage), `prefers-color-scheme`, `prefers-reduced-motion`, and
keyboard-navigable dialogs.
